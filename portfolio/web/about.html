About

A little about how I think

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at Kamaraj College of Engineering and Technology, graduating in 2028. I'm drawn to problems that sit at the intersection of pattern and computation — whether that's recreating a traditional Kolam with code, keeping a farmer's harvest cool with clay and sensors, or finding the shortest path through a network that doesn't want to be simplified.

What keeps me curious

Most of my project work starts the same way: I notice a system that already works — a piece of folk geometry, a rural cold-storage trick, a routing heuristic — and ask what it looks like once you write it down as an algorithm. That question has taken me from Smart India Hackathon proposals to a research paper on Kolam symmetry, and from a clay-pot cooling pod to a heritage-restoration platform.

Outside of coursework, I volunteer with NSS and TQI, mentor school students through the Happy Reading Project, and represent student communities as a campus ambassador for a few organisations I believe in.
